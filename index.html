Semiconductors in 2026: The AI Chip Boom

Why AI is changing the chip industry, what it means for South Korea, and what may happen next.

Creator: Rahul RauniyarMaterial verified: October 14, 2026Reading time: 5 minutes
How to read this briefing: Reported data describes something that has already happened. Forecasts and industry expectations describe what may happen. Forecasts can change.

The big picture

AI systems need powerful processors and fast memory. This is driving much of the expected market growth.

US$1.51 trillion

WSTS forecast for the global semiconductor market in 2026. This would be 90% growth compared with 2025. It is not the final confirmed total for the full year.

Four terms to know

Click the button to show or hide the definitions.

1. Semiconductor — 반도체

A material used to control electrical signals. Semiconductors are essential parts of phones, cars, and computers.

한국어: 전기 신호를 제어하는 데 사용되는 물질입니다. 스마트폰, 자동차, 컴퓨터 등의 핵심 부품입니다.

2. AI semiconductor — AI 반도체

A chip designed to perform calculations for artificial intelligence. It helps AI systems learn and respond.

한국어: 인공지능에 필요한 계산을 수행하며 AI 시스템의 학습과 응답을 돕는 반도체입니다.

3. Memory semiconductor — 메모리 반도체

A chip that stores data. DRAM and NAND are common types used in computers, phones, and data centers.

한국어: 데이터를 저장하는 반도체입니다. DRAM과 NAND가 대표적이며 컴퓨터, 스마트폰, 데이터센터 등에 사용됩니다.

4. HBM — 고대역폭 메모리

High Bandwidth Memory stacks memory chips and moves large amounts of data quickly. It is important for advanced AI systems.

한국어: HBM은 여러 메모리 칩을 쌓아 대량의 데이터를 빠르게 전달하는 기술입니다. 첨단 AI 시스템에 중요합니다.

Industry outlook: What could happen next?

There are strong opportunities, but growth is not guaranteed.

Growth factors

  • AI investment: More data centers may increase demand for processors and fast memory.
  • HBM demand: AI systems need memory that moves data quickly.
  • South Korea: Korean memory makers may benefit from demand for HBM and server memory.

한국어: AI 데이터센터 투자와 HBM 및 서버용 메모리 수요가 성장을 이끌 수 있습니다. 한국의 메모리 기업들은 이러한 수요의 혜택을 받을 수 있습니다.

Uncertainties and risks

  • AI companies may change or reduce investment plans.
  • Limited memory supply could raise costs for phone and computer makers.
  • Production capacity and global economic conditions may change the outlook.

한국어: AI 기업의 투자 변화, 메모리 공급 부족에 따른 비용 상승, 생산 능력과 세계 경제 상황이 시장 전망에 영향을 줄 수 있습니다.
